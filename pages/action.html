Take Action

Small, realistic changes you can do to help

Five ways to reduce your footprint

  • Reduce energy use at home – turn off lights and devices you're not using, and try washing clothes at a lower temperature.
  • Recycle and reduce waste – sort your recycling properly and try to avoid single-use plastic where you can.
  • Choose sustainable travel – walk, cycle or use public transport instead of the car for short journeys.
  • Reduce meat and dairy consumption – try a few plant-based meals a week.
  • Support renewable energy – ask about switching to a renewable energy tariff at home.
