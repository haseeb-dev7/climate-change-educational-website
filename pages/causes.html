Causes of Climate Change

Whats actually causing the crisis?

Burning fossil fuels

Coal, oil and gas are burned to generate electricity, power vehicles and heat buildings. This releases carbon dioxide, a greenhouse gas that traps heat in the atmosphere. Fossil fuels are the single biggest source of the greenhouse gases causing climate change.

Deforestation

Trees absorb carbon dioxide as they grow. When forests are cut down for farming, building or timber, that stored carbon is released back into the atmosphere, and there are fewer trees left to absorb it in future.

Agriculture and livestock farming

Farming animals such as cattle produces methane, a greenhouse gas far more powerful than carbon dioxide over the short term. Farmland also often replaces forest, adding to the problem.

Landfill waste and methane

Rubbish sent to landfill breaks down and releases methane as it decomposes. Reducing waste and recycling more can cut down on these emissions.
