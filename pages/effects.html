Effects of Climate Change

How a changing climate affects people and the planet

Rising sea levels and coastal flooding

As ice sheets and glaciers melt and oceans warm, sea levels rise. This increases the risk of flooding for towns and cities near the coast, and some low-lying areas are already losing land to the sea.

More extreme weather

Heatwaves, storms, and heavy rainfall are becoming more frequent and more intense, including here in the UK, according to Met Office data.

Loss of biodiversity and habitats

Changing temperatures and habitats put pressure on wildlife, and some species are struggling to adapt quickly enough.

Impact on food and water security

Droughts and unpredictable weather make it harder to grow crops and access clean water in some parts of the world, affecting food supplies and prices.
