Our mission

Think and Grow creates free educational resources for secondary schools. This website was built to help students understand climate change: what causes it, what effects it has, and what we can all do about it.

It's designed to be used either in Science, Geography or PSHE lessons, or by students researching independently, and is built to be accessible for students of all abilities aged 14 to 18.
